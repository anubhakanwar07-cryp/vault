Senior Product Manager — Payments Platform, Ledgerly (Growth stage fintech, Mumbai)

Ledgerly powers payments for 40,000 small businesses across India. Our Payments Platform team builds UPI collections, payouts and reconciliation used by every product in the company.

Responsibilities
- Own the payments platform roadmap: UPI AutoPay, payouts, and settlement reconciliation
- Reduce payment failure rates and improve success rates across banks and PSPs
- Work closely with engineering on API design, reliability and scale
- Drive compliance work with RBI guidelines together with legal and risk teams
- Partner with merchant-facing product teams who build on top of the platform
- Define and track platform KPIs: success rate, latency, settlement time, cost per transaction

Requirements
- 5+ years of product management, with at least 2 years on payments, fintech or platform/API products
- Deep understanding of UPI, card networks, or payment gateway infrastructure
- Experience writing technical specs and working with backend engineering teams
- Strong data skills — you can write SQL and build your own dashboards
- Excellent stakeholder management across risk, compliance and business teams

Good to have
- Experience with KYC / onboarding flows
- Prior experience in a B2B SaaS company
- MBA or engineering degree
