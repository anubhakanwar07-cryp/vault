Product Analyst — Cartwheel Commerce (late-stage e-commerce, Gurugram)

Cartwheel is one of India's largest fashion e-commerce platforms. The Product Analytics team partners with product managers to decide what to build and to measure whether it worked.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain dashboards for product health metrics (conversion, AOV, returns)
- Design and analyse A/B experiments with product and engineering teams
- Perform deep-dive analyses on user behaviour to identify opportunities and root causes
- Define metrics and instrumentation requirements for new features
- Present insights to product leadership with clear recommendations

Requirements
- 1–3 years of experience in product analytics, business analytics or data science
- Advanced SQL; experience with large datasets (BigQuery, Redshift or Snowflake)
- Experience with experimentation and statistics (hypothesis testing, power analysis)
- Proficiency in a BI tool such as Tableau, Looker or Metabase
- Python or R for analysis

Preferred
- E-commerce or marketplace experience
- Experience with dbt or data modelling
- Bachelor's degree in engineering, statistics, economics or a related field
