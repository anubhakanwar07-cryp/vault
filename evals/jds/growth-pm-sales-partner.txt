Growth Product Manager — Admissions

We help working adults enrol in career courses. Our admissions engine is a partnership between product, marketing and a 200-person sales team. You will own growth across the enrolment funnel.

What you'll do
- Partner closely with sales leadership to improve lead-to-enrolment conversion.
- Diagnose funnel drops across millions of leads and turn findings into experiments and process changes.
- Build business cases for new launches and present them to the founders.
- Equip the sales team with tools and insights that help reps sell better.
- Run market research to find and size new segments.

What we're looking for
- 1–3 years in product, growth or strategy
- Strong funnel analytics and SQL on large lead datasets
- Experience partnering with sales teams and sales enablement
- Business cases and executive communication
- Market research and competitive analysis

Nice to have
- A/B testing
- Edtech experience
