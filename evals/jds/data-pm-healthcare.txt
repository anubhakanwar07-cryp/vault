Product Manager, Data & Insights — CareGrid Health (enterprise healthcare SaaS, Pune / Remote)

CareGrid provides hospital operations software to 300 hospitals. The Data & Insights team builds analytics products that help hospital administrators reduce wait times and improve bed utilisation.

You will
- Own the analytics product: dashboards, alerts and forecasting features for hospital administrators
- Work with data engineers and data scientists to ship forecasting models (bed occupancy, staffing)
- Translate messy operational problems into clear product requirements
- Ensure data privacy and compliance with healthcare regulations (HIPAA-equivalent, DPDP Act)
- Conduct customer interviews with hospital administrators and nurses

We are looking for
- 3+ years of product management experience with data or analytics products
- Comfort with data modelling concepts and SQL
- Experience working with data scientists on ML or forecasting features
- Experience selling to or working with enterprise customers

Nice to have
- Healthcare domain experience
- Experience with BI tools (Power BI, Tableau)
